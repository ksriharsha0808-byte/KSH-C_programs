Your Cozy Digital Travel Journal

TravelMate was inspired by vintage paper passports, washi-tape scrapbooks, and the tactile joy of sticking polaroids onto cream stationery after a breezy day exploring cobblestone alleys.

Unlike rigid spreadsheets, TravelMate keeps planning delightfully human: colorful stickers, day-by-day morning pastries, afternoon museums, and relaxed evenings under fairy lights.

🥐 Slow Travel: Focus on memories, not checklists.
🎨 Scrapbook Aesthetics: Pastel hues, stamps & polaroids.
🌸 100% Client-Side: Lightweight, responsive, zero backend required.

Happy wandering & safe travels!
